# AirWatch.ai

**AirWatch.ai** is a web-based application for real-time air quality monitoring and prediction, powered by Gemini AI technology. The system provides accurate air pollution data and forecasts, along with timely alerts to pedestrians, residents living near roadways, and businesses operating along major roads.

The goal of this project is to contribute to public health and urban environmental safety by reducing exposure to harmful air pollutants.

## Features

- **Real-time Air Quality Monitoring**  
  Continuously tracks and displays current air quality levels based on environmental data.

- **AI-Powered Forecasting**  
  Uses Gemini AI models to predict changes in air quality and identify potential pollution events in advance.

- **Proactive Alerts**  
  Sends notifications to users about hazardous air conditions and recommends protective actions.

- **Web-Based Interface**  
  Designed with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to ensure a responsive and accessible user experience.

## Technologies Used

- **JavaScript** – Interactive client-side functionality  
- **HTML5** – Semantic structure for web pages  
- **CSS3** – Styling and layout  
- **Gemini AI** – Air quality prediction and data analysis

## Project Objectives

- Improve public awareness of air quality conditions  
- Reduce exposure to pollution in densely populated or high-traffic areas  
- Support healthier decision-making for individuals and businesses
